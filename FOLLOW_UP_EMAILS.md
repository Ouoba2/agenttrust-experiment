# Follow-Up Emails Strategy

Use these templates if you don't get a reply. 
**Rule:** Always reply to your ORIGINAL email so they have the context in the thread.

---

## Day 3: The Gentle Bump
*Send if no reply after 3 business days. Keep it ultra-short.*

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```text
Hi [Name],

Just floating this to the top of your inbox. I know you're busy, but I'd still love to hear your thoughts on how [Company] handles agent trust.

Would 15 minutes next Tuesday or Wednesday work for a quick chat?

Thanks,
Steve
```

---

## Day 7: The "Context" Bump
*Send if no reply after 7 days. Adds a tiny bit of context without pitching.*

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```text
Hi [Name],

I'm currently speaking with a few leaders in the agentic space to map out how the industry is tackling agent verification and trust. Your perspective at [Company] would be incredibly valuable to this research.

No sales pitch, just a 15-minute exchange of ideas. Let me know if you're open to it.

Best,
Steve
```

---

## Day 10: The "Breakup" (Final Attempt)
*Send if no reply after 10 days. Leaves the door open but stops chasing. Often triggers a reply because of FOMO.*

**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

```text
Hi [Name],

I assume the timing isn't right or this isn't a priority for [Company] right now, so I won't follow up again.

If agent trust and verification ever become a bigger focus for your team, I'd love to chat in the future. Keep up the great work with [Company]!

Best,
Steve
```
